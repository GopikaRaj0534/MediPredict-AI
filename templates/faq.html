How the predictions work, and what they don't do.

No. MediPredict AI is a screening tool that estimates risk based on patterns learned from health survey data. It cannot replace a blood test, physical exam, or a doctor's judgment. Treat a "high risk" result as a prompt to get properly tested, not as a confirmed condition.

Each module (Diabetes, Heart Disease, Stroke) uses a machine learning model trained on the CDC's BRFSS health survey data. You answer a set of lifestyle and health questions, the model compares your answers to patterns it learned from thousands of past records, and it returns a risk category plus a confidence score.

It's how strongly the model's internal probabilities favored the result it gave — not a measure of medical certainty. A 60% confidence "high risk" result is worth taking seriously, but so is a 95% one; both mean "get checked," just with different strength of signal.

Every form with a BMI field includes a built-in calculator. Enter your height and weight (metric or imperial) and it's calculated for you automatically.

Your prediction history is tied to your account and only visible to you when logged in. This is a student/demonstration project, not a clinically certified system — avoid entering information you wouldn't want stored in a learning project's database.

Each result page shows tailored next-step suggestions. In general: book an appointment with a doctor and mention which screening flagged a risk, so they know which tests to prioritize.
